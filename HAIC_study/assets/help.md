## Information on Data Collection During This Study

Thank you for participating in our study! Below, you will find detailed information about how your data will be handled during this research.

### Data Processing
Your personal data will be processed by the Technical University of Munich, represented by Nils Ness, as part of this study. Only the organizers of the survey will have access to your personal data.

### Revoking Consent
You can revoke your consent at any time without affecting the legality of the processing carried out based on your consent prior to revocation (Art. 6 para. 1 lit. a GDPR). Revoking your consent may result in your inability to participate in the survey. To revoke your consent, please contact us at [nils.ness@tum.de](mailto:nils.ness@tum.de).

### Data Storage and Processing
The survey is conducted using Revisit as part of the tum.de tenant's commissioned data processing. You can verify this via the "Data protection and cookies" link at the bottom of this page. The survey results are stored in Germany.

### Your Rights
Under legal requirements, you have the right to:
- Access your data
- Request rectification or erasure of your data
- Restrict or object to the processing of your data
- Request data portability

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Bavarian State Commissioner for Data Protection.

### Questions or Concerns
If you have any questions about this study or your data, please contact Nils Ness at [nils.ness@tum.de](mailto:nils.ness@tum.de) or our data protection officer.

Thank you for your participation and trust in our research!
